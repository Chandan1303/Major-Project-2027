===============================================================================
WHY MEDIAN (not MODE)?
===============================================================================

MODE: ❌ Doesn't work for continuous CV scores
      - CV scores: 0.8542, 0.8623, 0.8501 (never repeat exactly)
      - Mode is for categorical data (colors, categories)

MEDIAN: ✅ Perfect for CV scores
      - Robust to outliers
      - Standard practice in ML research
      - Recommended for agricultural/real-world data

MEAN: ⚠️ Traditional but not robust
      - One bad fold can skew results
      - Less reliable for noisy data

===============================================================================
TECHNICAL DETAILS
===============================================================================

Cross-Validation Setup:
  - Method: KFold
  - Folds: 5
  - Shuffle: True
  - Random State: 42 (reproducible)

Aggregation Function:
  - Primary: np.median(cv_scores)
  - Secondary: np.mean(cv_scores) [for comparison]
  - Spread: scipy.stats.iqr(cv_scores)

Metrics Evaluated:
  - R² (coefficient of determination)
  - RMSE (root mean squared error)
  - MAE (mean absolute error)
